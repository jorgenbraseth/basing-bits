Basing bits

Dead trees, skulls, ribcages, barrels, crates, fences, shipping containers, rocks and textured base toppers for 28mm miniatures. Everything is generated in your browser and exported as printable STL.

Piece

Gnarled dead tree with roots, knots and broken branches. About 35–50 mm tall at the default height.

Shape

Base

Shape

High angularity gives fractured desert stone with flat faces. Low angularity gives rounded, weathered stones.

Scale

1:40 is slightly heroic for 28mm, so the bones read well on the table. Strict 28mm is about 1:56.

Mesh detail

Fine takes a few times longer and makes larger files.

Viewport

Export

Files come as a .zip with the STL inside, named with model and seed so you can make the same piece again.

Scene

    Add a piece to start a scene, for example a base topper, then switch tabs and add rocks or props on top. Select a piece in this list to move or rotate it.

    Use the Move and Rotate tool at the top of the view: drag the arrows to move along an axis, or the rings to turn. Lock to one axis with X, Y or Z in the tool (or the X, Y, Z keys; press again to unlock). Keys: M move, R rotate, arrows nudge 1 mm (Shift: 5 mm), Q and E turn 15°, Delete removes.

    The scene downloads as one STL with every piece in place. Pieces that overlap are merged by the slicer when it prints.
